# Privacy Policy for Meet Transcript

**Effective date: August 15, 2026**

Meet Transcript is a Chrome extension that captures captions displayed during Google Meet sessions and helps users organize, review, highlight, export, and optionally save transcripts to their own Google Drive account.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Meet Transcript processes, how that information is used, and the choices available to users.

## 1. Information We Process

Meet Transcript may process the following information when the extension is used:

* Caption and transcript text displayed during a Google Meet session.
* Participant display names associated with captions.
* Meeting information, such as the meeting title, meeting code, date, and timestamps.
* User-created highlights, bookmarks, action items, and notes.
* Extension preferences, campaign information, and selected Google Drive folder identifiers.
* Google OAuth authorization information required to save files to the user’s Google Drive.

Meet Transcript does not record or access microphone audio, camera video, passwords, financial information, health information, precise location, or general browsing history.

## 2. How Information Is Used

The information processed by Meet Transcript is used only to provide its core functionality:

* Displaying captions as a real-time transcript.
* Organizing transcript content by speaker and timestamp.
* Creating highlights, notes, bookmarks, and action items.
* Saving meeting history locally.
* Exporting transcript files.
* Saving transcript files to a Google Drive folder selected by the user.
* Restoring pending transcripts and retrying failed Google Drive saves.

The information is not used for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness assessment, lending, or purposes unrelated to the extension’s functionality.

## 3. Data Storage

Transcript data, meeting history, highlights, notes, and extension settings may be stored locally in the user’s browser.

When the user enables Google Drive saving, transcript files are uploaded directly to the user’s own Google Drive account using Google APIs. Users control the destination folder and the files stored in their Google Drive.

Meet Transcript does not operate a developer-controlled server for receiving or storing transcript content.

## 4. Google Account and Google Drive Access

Meet Transcript uses Google OAuth through the Chrome Identity API to request permission to save transcript files to Google Drive.

The extension:

* Does not receive or store the user’s Google password.
* Uses authorization only to provide Google Drive features requested by the user.
* Does not access unrelated Google Drive files.
* Does not share Google authorization information with advertisers or data brokers.

Users can revoke the extension’s Google account access at any time through their Google Account permissions page.

## 5. Data Sharing and Transfers

Meet Transcript does not sell user data.

Meet Transcript does not transfer user data to third parties except when necessary to provide a feature explicitly requested by the user, such as saving a transcript to the user’s own Google Drive account.

No transcript data is shared with advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored data remains in the browser until the user deletes it, clears the extension’s local data, or removes the extension.

Users can delete locally stored transcripts and settings from the Meet Transcript interface.

Files saved to Google Drive remain there until the user deletes them from their Google Drive account. Removing local extension data does not automatically delete files previously saved to Google Drive.

## 7. Data Security

Meet Transcript uses secure HTTPS connections when communicating with Google APIs.

The extension is designed to process only the information necessary to provide its stated transcript management functionality. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. User Control and Consent

Meet Transcript processes Google Meet captions only while the extension is active on a Google Meet page.

Users can:

* Pause or stop transcript capture.
* Choose whether to save transcripts to Google Drive.
* Export or delete transcripts.
* Disable Google Drive auto-save.
* Revoke Google account access.
* Clear all locally stored data.
* Uninstall the extension at any time.

Users are responsible for ensuring they have any consent required by applicable laws or organizational policies before capturing or storing meeting transcripts.

## 9. Children’s Privacy

Meet Transcript is not specifically directed to children under the age required by applicable law, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the extension’s functionality or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

## 11. Limited Use Compliance

Meet Transcript’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/limited-use), including the Limited Use requirements.
